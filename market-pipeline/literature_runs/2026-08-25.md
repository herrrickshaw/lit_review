### Deep-read — PIB Explainer 155010 (Min. of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, 11 Aug 2025)
Fetched directly; the URL 403s to plain fetchers and needs a browser user-agent.
Carries 78.41 crore applications insured, ₹1.83 lakh crore claims paid since 2016,
enrolment +32% (3.17→4.19 crore), and the full premium-sharing rule: farmer pays a
capped 2%/1.5%/5%, governments bear "95% to 98.5%" on a 50:50 split (90:10 for
NE/Himalayan states).

**The finding is what the release omits**: it headlines the payout but never states
what farmers paid in. By the scheme's own rates the farmer funds a low-single-digit
percentage of actuarial premium, so nearly all of the ₹1.83 lakh crore is public money
routed through insurers — insurance in name, subsidy in structure. Same framing as the
published `who-collects-indias-gst-and-who`.

series is not confirmed obtainable — pmfby.gov.in returns an identical 6,842-byte SPA

argument survives without it (rule + totals are enough); anything state-level does not.
Release is also ~15 months stale and must be re-based before writing.
