**Deep-read — 0810.1922 (Daniel, Sornette & Wöhrmann; submitted 2008-10-10, JPM 36(1) Fall
2009).** The oldest paper in the register and the most useful Friday hit so far, because it
aims at the half of survivorship this repo has *not* already disarmed. The familiar version
is strategy-side — picking from a universe of survivors. This is **benchmark-side**:
evaluating against an index whose constituents are taken from the *end* of the test period.
CRSP 1926–2006, running top-500 US caps, S&P 500 benchmark; bias **up to 8%/annum**, and it
also **overstates Sharpe and understates peak-to-valley drawdown**.

**Doing-right, verified by measurement rather than by comment.** The India panel is
genuinely survivorship-free: on `~/.cache/market-warehouse/ohlcv_adj/IN` (path from
`signal_tracker.py:100`), **513 symbols in `year=2016.parquet` are absent from
`year=2026.parquet`**, and DHFL, DENABANK and DISHMAN are each present in 2016 and gone by
2026. The strategy side is disarmed in writing too — `smallcap_screener.py:30-38` refuses
to backtest on the real Smallcap 250 constituents at all, because NSE publishes only the
current list; `band_sip_backtest.py:20-26` carries delisting as two explicit bounds.

**🔴 Gap — the benchmark side leaks, and the leak is live.** `signal_tracker.py:436` falls
back to `wide.median(axis=1)`, and `:417-428` records that this fires for India (NIFTYBEES
stopped updating after an equity-only bhavcopy filter). That series is used as a *level*:
`:473-474` chains a return off it, `:479` reports `xret_pct = ret - bret`. A median of price
levels across a changing cross-section is not an index — not survivor-biased (delisted names
lose their bars, which is correct) but **composition-drifting**: the same panel runs **1,654
symbols in 2016 to 7,073 in 2026**, so the median moves when membership changes, with no
market move behind it.

**This is the unfixed half of a known bug, not a re-report.** The session log
(`LITERATURE_REGISTER.md:140`) records this fallback producing a fabricated **+46.77%**
"vs bench" for India on 2026-08-22/23, "replaced with a real NIFTYBEES+NIFTY50 splice". The
splice went into the **research script** (`research/stock_pick_performance.py:139-178`,
rescaled at the seam). The **live tracker was never changed**. So the mechanism was
diagnosed a month ago, fixed where it was found, and left in the module that writes the
ledger everything else is graded against.

**Declined:** the paper's random-strategy remedy — `rloo_rescore.py:271,281` already scores
picks against a leave-one-out baseline of other eligible names in the same liquidity tier,
a matched-constraint null with the constraint that actually binds here.

**Queued:** BACKTEST_QUEUE item 28 — port the spliced benchmark into `signal_tracker.py`,
replace the fallback with a return-chained equal-weight index rather than a median of
levels, and **first** measure in `research/` what the median has been reporting. Falsifiable
prediction stated: the bias is *upward* (new small-cap listings enter below incumbent price
levels, dragging the median down and inflating `xret_pct`), which would make the recorded
"median vs benchmark −2.74%" better than the truth rather than worse.
