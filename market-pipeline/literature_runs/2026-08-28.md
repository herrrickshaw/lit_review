**The finding.** India has **23.14 million minor irrigation schemes; 21.93 million (94.8%)
are groundwater**, not canals. **96.6% are privately owned** (98.3% of groundwater schemes),
and of the 60.2% with a single source of finance, **79.5% were funded by the farmer's own
savings**. Against that, the AERR abstract reports that across 20 states, 1981-82 to
2019-20, rising public capital formation in irrigation "has barely corresponded" with net
irrigated area, potential utilised, or financial recovery. The census explains why both hold:
the state capitalises the ~5% it owns; the country irrigates itself off the rest, out of
pocket. Every census figure is verbatim from the PIB release, fetched in full (browser UA
required — plain fetchers get 403).
