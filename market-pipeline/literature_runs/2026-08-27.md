### Baldi-Lanfranchi — Transaction-cost-aware Factors (EPFL/SFI, 17 May 2024)
Standard factors rebalance *fully* to target each period (transaction-cost-unaware);
TCA factors rebalance *partially* at an optimized intensity τ. Momentum is the worked
case: gross 0.64%/mo, TCU costs 63bps/mo, **net 0.01%/mo** — costs eat all of it. TCA
momentum pays 30bps/mo, netting **22× more** and +0.19 annualized net Sharpe. Net Sh²
improves 28-150%; the Barillas-Shanken six-factor model has the best gross Sh² (2.25)
but drops **80% after costs**. Grade: medium-strong (sample period and universe not
disclosed on the pages fetched — a limit of what was read, not a claim about the paper).

Why it matters here: this repo measured momentum 12-1 at **-14.11% vs bench (IN)** and
**-15.08% (US)** on 2026-08-23 and filed it as "no edge" — using the full-rebalance
form the paper says is the problem. Repo is on the correct *net* side already
(`walk_forward_backtest.py:156`, `backtest_screeners.py:176`, both 0.2% round-trip), but
`grep -rniE 'trading_intensity|partial.rebal|turnover_penalt|cost.aware'` returns **zero
non-vendored hits** — its only cost mitigation is the watchlist's >5-session eviction, a
*frequency* heuristic the paper's footnote 1 argues is dominated by intensity
optimization. Full TCA is out of scope (no weight vector, no execution layer), so
**item 16 queues only the reduced form**: re-run momentum 12-1 with a hold-through band
and report gross/turnover/net for each width.

cross-section** (`axis=1`, `:309-310`), applied symmetrically to picks and peers.
