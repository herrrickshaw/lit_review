~5,000 mined factors from nine methods + Alpha101 on S&P 100 / HSI / CSI 300 / FTSE 100 /
Nikkei 225 (782 tickers), train 2017–21, val 2022–23, test 2024–25, 10 bps, 20-day rebalance.

  (Table 8: GP 3.7→−2.0, R&D-Agent 6.5→−1.6). Hand-written Alpha101 goes 2.6→3.0.
- Long-only survives costs to 50 bps; L/S falls to zero (App. E.3). Longer holds help.
- No paradigm dominates; Alpha101 is competitive with every mined pool.
- **Doing-right:** per-market costs (`confusion_matrix_eval.py:34`), deflated Sharpe
  (`deflated_sharpe.py:7-13`), EW baseline (`train_ppo_factor_weights.py:147`), breakeven
  cost for ETF schemes only (`etf_builder.py:290`).
- **Gap:** no style neutralisation anywhere (grep hits only bad-print neutralisation,
  `stock_utils.py:171`); every cost is a fixed scalar, never swept.
