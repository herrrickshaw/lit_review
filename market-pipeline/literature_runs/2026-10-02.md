### 2603.19380 (deep-read; extracted from the arXiv PDF text)
- **Method.** The paper rebuilds membership from 2,459 NSE bhavcopies (2016-09 to 2025-09)
  and ranks names 151–400 using traded value as a stand-in for market cap.
- **Result.** It compares two daily-rebalanced equal-weight portfolios. Survivors return
  26.17% a year against 21.23% for all names ever in the index (+4.94pp). Sharpe is 1.160
  against 1.063. Costs are not modelled; the paper says so itself.
- **Grade: moderate-to-weak.**
  - The "100% (252/252)" reconstruction accuracy is checked only in-sample, against
    today's list.
  - The "85-90%" historical accuracy has no ground truth behind it.
  - The significance claim contradicts itself: "exceeds 97.8% of resamples" implies
    p≈0.022, but the paper prints "p < 0.001".
- **Doing-right (verified):**
  - `code/python_files/smallcap_screener.py:20-36` already validates only on the price
    panel that includes delisted names, approximates the band point-in-time by turnover
    rank, and refuses to backtest the current list.
  - It also records that a market-cap reconstruction matches today's list at only 71%
    (`:34`), which makes the paper's 100% look tuned.
  - `price_band_migration.py:23` reports survivorship rather than dropping it.
- **Gap:** none that can be acted on. True point-in-time membership needs NSE
  index-maintenance notices, and the repo already flags that (`smallcap_screener.py:34-36`).
