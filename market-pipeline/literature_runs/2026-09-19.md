**Deep-read 1: 1102.4076, Livan, Alfarano & Scalas, "The fine structure of spectral properties
for random correlation matrices" (2011-02-20; Phys. Rev. E 84, 016113).** Deliberately old — six
modern spectral papers are now in this register and none supplies this: a block of N̄ assets with
mean internal correlation ρ deposits **N̄−1 eigenvalues at exactly 1−ρ** plus one at N̄ρ+(1−ρ).
The bulk is superposed cluster structure, not noise. Checked on S&P500 (396 names, T=3400,
1996–2009) and FTSE350 (243, T=1423, 2005–2010); their S&P block was 7 energy names at ρ=0.712,
predicted small eigenvalue 0.288.

🔴 **This lands on queue item 9 and shows it cannot run as written.** Item 9 asks for
`m⁻ = count of eigenvalues below (1−√(n/T))²` from `market_correlation_scan.py`'s nightly matrix.
That matrix is full-universe — 2,225 resolved NSE symbols per the module's own docstring
(`:220-222`) — at `period: str = "1y"` (`:99`, default, threaded `:261`, `:283`, `:295`, `:362`).
**n/T ≈ 8.9.** The lower MP edge (1−√8.9)² = 3.92 sits above most of the spectrum, ~1,975
eigenvalues are identically zero, and m⁻ degenerates to ≈ n − T — it would track how many tickers
resolved that night, not the market. Its source paper 2608.09641 ran n/T ≈ 0.38/0.20. The research
panel is no better: `correlation_regime_spectrum.py:34-35` is 150 names over a 60-day window,
n/T 2.1–2.5 across the 299 rows of the CSV (n_symbols 126–150, re-verified today).
