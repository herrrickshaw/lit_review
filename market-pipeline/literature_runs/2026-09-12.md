**Deep-read 1 — 2309.17219 (Bongiorno & Challet, 2023-09-29), "Covariance matrix filtering
and portfolio optimisation: the Average Oracle vs Non-Linear Shrinkage and all the variants
of DCC-NLS".** This is the *source* of the "Average Oracle" baseline queue item 18 already
names but knew only second-hand through 2507.01918's table. Reported: AO1200 **Sharpe 1.229
at turnover 0.068** vs DCC-QIS **0.894-0.895 at turnover 1.307-1.308** — ~19x the turnover
for a lower Sharpe, the same failure mode 2507.01918 showed. Method now specified: keep the
current eigenvectors, replace eigenvalues with a fixed set bootstrap-calibrated once on an
earlier sample (random draws of periods *and* assets; each draw takes the covariance of its
"future" period against the adjacent past period's eigenvectors, then averages). No
training, no GPU.

this repo has from `df.corr()` on the ten factor columns, eigendecomposed at `:42-54`. The
PnL-side matrix has never been computed.
