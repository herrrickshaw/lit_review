**2610.04040** (Chen et al., 2026-10-02). **Medium as a method, weak as a result.**
- **Method:** hold fixed the count of each action change (flat→long, …). Randomly reassign
  those changes across eligible events. The mean payoff of the reassignments is the
  *composition benchmark*; observed value minus that benchmark is *selection value*.
- **Results:**
  - On 723 earnings events at 44 US firms, 2020–24, with 40 bps costs: +15.2 bps gross
    splits into +25.8 composition and −10.6 selection (p = 0.63).
  - In a semi-synthetic check, false skill attribution falls from 11.6% to 5.3%.
- **Doing-right (verified):** per-signal-date excess returns
  (`signal_tracker.py:644-655`) and a benchmark staleness check (`:586-612`).
- **Gap (verified):**
  - The benchmark is a cap-weighted index (`signal_tracker.py:174`, NIFTYBEES/SPY), so a
    universe tilt counts as skill.
  - EU/JP/KR have no benchmark (`:483-487`).
  - There is no matched random-pick baseline anywhere. A grep for
    permutation/placebo/random draws finds only an unrelated Sharpe bootstrap
    (`r_analysis.py:399`).
